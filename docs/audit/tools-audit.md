# Tools audit — 1 November 2026

Baseline: `origin/main` at `1f23808`, version **1.7.0-beta.1**. This is the Part 1 report, before any production fixes on this branch. Later drawing corrections are recorded separately in `science-check.md`; the findings below deliberately retain the original evidence.

## Method and coverage

The built Electron app was driven off-screen through Playwright and `e2e/lib.mjs`, with disposable profiles and lesson libraries. The renderer viewport was measured, not inferred from the native window size: **1280×720, 1366×768 and 1920×1080**, board and laptop layouts, white and green chalk surfaces. UI appearance followed the surface. Evidence lives in `D:\Projects 2\whiteboard\audit-shots`, outside the repository.

`e2e/tools-audit.mjs` opens the 43 More tiles in board mode, the corresponding laptop sheets, every available subject tab, and every cupboard part. Each part is taken out using its real button. All setting choices, both ends of each range, switches, and each on-part control are exercised; then the part is moved, resized, rotated and undone. Fixture placement and selection use the debug hook; actual settings use their UI, on-part controls and resize/rotation use pointer gestures, and movement uses the keyboard. A blocked touch target is recorded before trying keyboard access. Files in `probes/disk-round-trips.json` record actual writes and reads through the lesson API, beyond the in-memory serialization checks.

Full-window screenshots are named by layout, width and surface. Each configuration's `coverage.json` records every action and any failure. The contact sheets are inspection aids; finding links below point to original app screenshots. The original seven workflow suites also passed: tools **19**, instruments **15**, classroom **28**, templates **25**, scale **25**, tables **33**, apparatus **31**; zero failures.

Completed coverage: **12/12 configurations, 696 part visits, 2,064 setting actions, 288 on-part control actions**. All 696 move/resize/rotate/undo sequences completed. A separate file-persistence sweep recreated each part with its recorded final setting values, a resized box and a nonzero rotation, then wrote/read/reopened it at each configuration (**696/696**); it checks parameters and geometry, allowing serialization's intentional 0.01-unit rounding. This is distinct from the in-memory round trip performed immediately after each original interaction sequence. The sole touch-access warning is finding 6, reproduced on both 1280-wide board surfaces and completed by keyboard. No renderer errors were logged. The six board configurations each opened all six subject tabs and all 43 More tiles; each layout/surface/size opened its 26 sheet routes.

The toolbar fitting 1280 wide, brush streak, side tabs, decorative pictures and radial menu are excluded, as requested. Native file pickers were opened and cancelled; the save dialog was supplied a temporary `.wbd` path. Minimise/Exit handlers were invoked with window actions intercepted so the audit could continue. This does not certify Windows dialog interaction, external imports, a physical panel, pen/palm behaviour, display scaling or real teaching acceptance. Subject tabs are a board-layout feature; the laptop uses Insert/Teach equivalents.

## Findings, worst first

1. **Concave lens loses the negative focal-length sign — high, incorrect teaching value.** Take out a Concave lens and inspect its label, then change Focal length. Its stored `f` is negative, but painting applies `Math.abs(q.f)`, so both converging and diverging lenses say `f = 150`. NCERT's Cartesian convention distinguishes them by sign. [Screenshot](../../../audit-shots/laptop-1280-light/part-concave-lens.png). Owner: permitted physics paint function. Reference: [NCERT Class X, §9.3.6](https://ncert.nic.in/textbook/pdf/jesc109.pdf).

2. **Zero force reads 0.1 N — high, incorrect teaching value.** Take out Force arrow and drag its tip back to the origin. The zero-length guard changes the magnitude to one before formatting, producing `0.1 N` instead of zero. [Screenshot](../../../audit-shots/probes/zero-force.png). Owner: permitted physics paint function.

3. **An empty vessel claims neutral pH — high, misleading measurement.** Take out a beaker, drag its liquid level to zero and enable Show the pH. It displays `pH 7.0` despite having no sample. An empty apparatus has no solution pH to measure. [Screenshot](../../../audit-shots/probes/empty-vessel-ph.png). Owner: permitted vessel paint; `chem.ts` also returns seven for zero volume and remains untouched.

4. **Microscope magnification choices do not match the displayed enlargement — high for quantitative use.** Focus the onion peel, switch from 100× to 400× and 1000×. Painting uses the stored visual factors 1, 2 and 3.4 directly, rather than ratios 1, 4 and 10. No scale bar or model qualification explains this. The objective also stops well above the slide. [100× view](../../../audit-shots/laptop-1280-light/state-microscope-mag-0.png), [1000× view](../../../audit-shots/laptop-1280-light/state-microscope-mag-2.png). Owner: permitted microscope paint; preserve the settings' stored values.

5. **Spring-balance pointer and number disagree — medium, wrong scale reading.** Set Mass hanging to 1000 g. The exact label says 9.8 N, but the pointer is drawn on the mark labelled 10. At 500 g it similarly lands on 5 while the value is 4.9 N. Maximum load also crowds the bottom reading plate. [Screenshot](../../../audit-shots/probes/spring-scale.png). Owner: permitted spring-balance paint.

6. **Small-part settings can be covered by the board toolbar — medium, touch control unavailable.** At 1280×720 in board layout, take out Evaporating dish, select Set, choose the liquid and indicator, then try Show the pH. The panel extends behind the bottom toolbar; the toolbar intercepts the click. Keyboard focus is a workaround, not a classroom touch solution. Both surfaces reproduce it. [White](../../../audit-shots/board-1280-light/error-evaporating-dish.png), [chalk](../../../audit-shots/board-1280-dark/error-evaporating-dish.png). 7. **DNA has floating base-pair rungs without continuous backbones — medium, missing defining structure.** Take out DNA and stop Turning. Individual end dots never join into two sugar-phosphate strands; antiparallel polarity is absent, and the angular spacing is not the NCERT approximation of ten pairs per turn. [Screenshot](../../../audit-shots/laptop-1280-light/part-dna.png). Owner: permitted DNA paint. Reference: [NCERT Class XII, §5.1.1](https://ncert.nic.in/textbook/pdf/lebo105.pdf).

8. **The leaf's lower cuticle seals the illustrated stoma — medium, incorrect anatomy.** Take out Inside a leaf. A continuous waxy band is painted beneath the guard cells and pore, closing the route to outside air. The drawing is specifically a dorsiventral dicot leaf but its generic name does not say that. 10. **Breathing model says lungs empty on expiration — medium, misleading wording.** Take out Breathing model with the rubber sheet released. The caption says “lungs empty”; lungs retain residual air. The pictured balloons and rubber sheet should also be distinguished from actual organs. 11. **Pendulum can paint outside its selection and into its own caption — medium, teaching diagram becomes awkward.** Set Length to 1 m and Swing to 30°, then start. With the old 330-unit drawing length, the bob can reach x≈315 plus its radius in a 300-unit part; near the bottom its radius enters the period plate. [Screenshot](../../../audit-shots/probes/pendulum-envelope.png). Owner: permitted pendulum paint. The period formula is the small-angle approximation, especially relevant at the allowed 30° maximum.

12. **A supposedly empty burette still has a coloured heel — medium, state and picture disagree.** Drain a burette completely. The liquid rectangle always includes an extra 18 units, leaving colour below the 50 mL graduation when the quantity is zero. The top reading plate also extends above the part's bounds. [Screenshot](../../../audit-shots/probes/empty-burette.png). Owner: permitted burette paint.

13. **Magnetic field lines have no direction and dwarf the selectable magnet — medium, incomplete teaching aid.** Enable Show field lines. The curves extend hundreds of units beyond the 260×70 object while the selectable box stays small; no arrow shows the external N-to-S direction or return path inside. [Screenshot](../../../audit-shots/probes/magnet-lines.png). Owner: permitted magnet paint; the force/compass solver stays with the lead.

14. **Balance readings embed undisclosed fixed-mass assumptions — medium, unsuitable for a quantitative density lesson.** Put an empty beaker on the balance, then an empty measuring cylinder. Both read exactly 90.0 g because `world.ts::massOf` assigns every vessel 90 g and every liquid 1 g/mL, regardless of shape or contents. This is a simulation assumption, not a calibrated model of those particular vessels. [Beaker](../../../audit-shots/probes/balance-beaker.png), [cylinder](../../../audit-shots/probes/balance-cylinder.png). Owner: lead (`world.ts`); no behaviour change here.

15. **Glassware and support drawings omit recognisable construction — low to medium, weak classroom recognition.** The measuring cylinder has no broad foot or pouring lip; the evaporating dish looks like blue transparent glass rather than a ceramic basin; the tripod has a strip instead of a gauze mesh; the microscope lacks a connected objective/stage path. These are visible at all three sizes, especially the small cupboard thumbnails. [Cylinder](../../../audit-shots/laptop-1280-light/part-measuring-cylinder.png), [dish](../../../audit-shots/laptop-1280-light/part-evaporating-dish.png), [tripod](../../../audit-shots/laptop-1280-light/part-tripod.png). Owner: permitted drawing functions.

## Behaviour limits for the lead and teacher

- Undoing the burette's **close-tap** correctly reopens it; the measured total volume stayed conserved. Undo does not rewind elapsed simulation time. Do not mistake this deliberate architecture for a loss-of-liquid bug. `probes/findings.json` keeps the before/after quantities.
- Vessel height is linear in volume even for conical and spherical vessels. It is a schematic level control, not a calibrated physical meniscus. Keep the numerical quantity explicit and have a teacher review quantitative usage before extending it.
- `world.ts` finds neighbouring vessels by bounding boxes, including after rotation. It does not model spilling, buoyancy, evaporation or density-dependent heating. `chem.ts` uses a simplified strong-acid/strong-base model at fixed pH conventions; dye mixing, weak acids and very dilute equilibrium are not validated chemistry.
- The electrical parts were all exercised, but their solver and paint are outside this branch's change scope. A resistor keeps the same colour bands when its resistance setting changes. [Example at 1000 Ω](../../../audit-shots/laptop-1280-light/state-resistor-r-1000.png). Ask the lead to make the bands agree with the value or clearly mark the drawing schematic.

No production file was changed for Part 1. No PR, merge or installation is part of this audit.
